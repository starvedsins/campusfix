## Design Context

### Users
Students and staff report campus faults. Technicians use phones in the field. Dispatchers and administrators use desktop computers to coordinate repairs.

### Brand Personality
Clear, calm, dependable. Give users confidence that their report has been recorded and that the next action is clear.

### Aesthetic Direction
A light campus service interface with a dark ink navigation rail, restrained green actions, gold campus details, generous page headings, and compact ticket tables. Use Manrope for headings and Public Sans for text. Use real status text as well as colour. Avoid decorative dashboards and motion that delays work.

### Design Principles
- Make the next action clear for each role.
- Use plain English and short sentences.
- Keep mobile forms and field controls easy to use.
- Meet WCAG 2.1 AA with visible focus and clear errors.
- Use space and type to group content. Keep decoration limited.
